## Activities built for every week
1. Vocabulary
2. Listen & Tap
3. Match
4. Phonics
5. Words to Know
6. Grammar
7. Read & Find
8. Quick Check
9. Wheel Spin (15 segments)

## Content basis
The weekly questions, reading titles/genres, full recovered vocabulary sets for Weeks 1–5, high-frequency words, phonics patterns, and language skills are grounded in the supplied myView 1.4 Unit 4 PDFs.

The practice passages and practice questions in the app are newly written for this app rather than copied from the textbook. The approved week-card art is original artwork made for this project rather than textbook art.

## Progress
Activity completion and Wheel Spin progress are stored locally in the browser with `localStorage`.
