# v0.25 — Match free choice

- Picture Match, Meaning Match, and Memory Match are all available immediately.
- Removed sequential unlocking between the three games.
- The three Match buttons now act as direct game selectors.
- Completed games receive a check mark while the other games remain playable.
- Students may play the three games in any order.
- Overall Match completion is still recorded once all three games have been completed.
- Each game continues to use its own six-word set, for 18 different words per full Match run.
